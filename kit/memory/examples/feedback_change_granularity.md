---
name: change-granularity
description: Prefers one bundled change for a cohesive revision over a chain of small ones
type: feedback
---

For a single cohesive revision, deliver it as one bundled change with one note of what moved, not a
chain of small ones.

**Why:** confirmed after I split a terminology update into five separate rounds and the person said
it was just churn - none of the pieces could be checked or sent on alone, so the split added review
overhead without value. This is a validated preference, not a one-off.

**How to apply:** when a change is one logical unit (a defined term renamed across every document, a
section moved, one new clause applied everywhere), keep it as one. Still split when parts are
genuinely independent, go out at different times, or carry different risk.
